# Cool Video: Product Gallery Videos and Autoplay

Cool Video adds YouTube, Vimeo and Dailymotion videos to product galleries.
Videos can be added, removed, enabled and positioned from the Modules section
of the product editor.

## Compatibility

- PrestaShop 1.7.x, 8.x and 9.x
- Classic and Hummingbird gallery adapters
- Independent fallback for custom themes that expose standard product hooks
- The PHP and MySQL/MariaDB versions supported by the installed PrestaShop release

## Installation

1. Open Module Manager in the PrestaShop back office.
2. Select the option to upload a module.
3. Choose `coolvideo.zip` and complete the installation.
4. Edit a product and open its Modules section to add a video URL.

Uninstalling the module also removes the table containing product-video
assignments.

The gallery position is shared by product images and videos and starts at `1`.
A video assigned to position `1` becomes the initial gallery item when the
product page opens.

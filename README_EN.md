# 🎬 CoolVideo – Product Gallery Video Module for PrestaShop 1.7 / 8 / 9

Lightweight and simple module that adds video directly to the product image gallery.  
Supports YouTube, Vimeo and MP4 files.  
Fully compatible with PrestaShop 1.7, 8 and 9.

## 📌 About CoolVideo

**CoolVideo** allows you to place a video inside the product image gallery — exactly where customers expect to see it.  
The video appears as a gallery item next to product photos, providing a more engaging presentation and increasing conversion rates.

The module is lightweight, fast and requires no theme modifications.
